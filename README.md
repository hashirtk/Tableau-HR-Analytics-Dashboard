📊 HR Analytics Dashboard (Tableau)
📌 Overview

This Tableau dashboard provides an in-depth analysis of key HR metrics to help organizations monitor workforce performance, track attrition trends, and identify factors influencing employee satisfaction. It brings together multiple HR dimensions into one interactive and easy-to-understand view.

🔍 Key Metrics & Insights

The dashboard covers several important HR indicators:

👥 Workforce Overview

Total Employee Count

Attrition Count

Active Employees

Average Employee Age

Attrition Rate

🏢 Department-Wise Attrition

Visual breakdown of attrition across HR, R&D, and Sales departments, helping identify areas with higher turnover.

📈 Employee Age Distribution

A detailed age-group analysis that highlights the concentration of employees across different ranges.

🌟 Job Satisfaction Rating

Heatmap showing satisfaction scores for each job role, helping managers understand engagement levels.

🎓 Education Field-Wise Attrition

Attrition trends across Life Sciences, Medical, Technical Degrees, Marketing, Human Resources, and Others.

⚧ Gender-Wise Attrition by Age Group

Comparison of male and female attrition across age segments to identify patterns and risk groups.

🎯 Purpose of the Dashboard

This dashboard helps HR teams and decision-makers:

Monitor employee retention and identify high-risk areas

Understand workforce demographics and distribution

Analyze job satisfaction to support engagement initiatives

Support data-driven HR planning and strategy development
